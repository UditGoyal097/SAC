Discussion Forum

Ask Your Doubt

Top 5 Questions and Answers

  • Question 1: What is the format of the Skit Smash event?
    Answer: The Skit Smash event will be a series of short performances by different groups, each showcasing their creative skits.
  • Question 2: What are the rules for the Cricket Tournament?
    Answer: The Cricket Tournament will follow standard T20 rules. Teams must consist of at least 11 players.
  • Question 3: How can I participate in the Poetry Reading event?
    Answer: Participants can sign up for the Poetry Reading event by contacting the event coordinator or signing up online.
  • Question 4: What topics will be covered in the Coding Mela?
    Answer: The Coding Mela will cover a range of topics including web development, data science, and competitive programming.
  • Question 5: Is there an age limit for Picasso's Art event?
    Answer: There is no age limit for Picasso's Art event. Everyone is welcome to participate and showcase their artistic talents.
